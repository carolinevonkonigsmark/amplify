# Amplify — Demo Voiceover Script

*Runtime target: ~2:20, matching the original. Screen cues in brackets — they follow the demo's nav order.*

---

**[Overview page — architecture]**

Today, AI enablement programs are manual, reactive, and require a lot of cross-functional context tracing. 10x changes that completely.

**[Click into AI Pulse]**

Our first layer is what we've nicknamed the AI Pulse. This is a proficiency intelligence layer, designed to continuously ingest AI usage signals from across the org and segment them by team, location, and seniority. That means when a new capability ships — or a team stalls — we know exactly which cohorts need enablement most, and can act immediately.

**[AI Pulse — switch between Adoption Mapping and Proficiency Matrix]**

Let's walk through how we go from a newly launched capability to targeted enablement in minutes.

**[Scenario Orchestrator — paste the brief, click Generate]**

Here's our capability brief — in this case, an announcement about computer-use agents. We jump straight into the Scenario Orchestrator and paste it in. The AI engine analyzes the content and generates a complete, targeted campaign — tailored to the specific skill gap and each cohort's proficiency profile. What used to take days of planning, stakeholder coordination, and content development now happens in a couple of hours.

**[Module Catalog, then Amplify Command Centre]**

The enablement team has a full view of every draft and published asset, and can monitor active and completed campaigns.

**[Teams Delivery]**

And here's what the employee actually sees. We spin up tailored simulations in minutes — not generic training, but content specific to the capability and their proficiency profile, delivered directly in Microsoft Teams.

**[Prompt Lab — click through the scenario]**

Employees engage with realistic workflow scenarios — like this one: a confidential board summary due in three hours. Every choice feeds into their AI proficiency profile, and the system continuously adapts to surface the enablement that drives the best outcomes.

**[Amplifyverse — home view, then switch to Galaxy Map]**

All of this lives in the Amplifyverse, our experiential portal. It offers two modes: a traditional LMS, or a gamified experience that leverages behavioral economics to drive voluntary learning. Badges, levels, and social proof create intrinsic motivation.

The Amplifyverse is designed to host everything — AI-generated video content, interactive simulations, choose-your-own-adventure scenarios, and prompt-craft challenges — all in one scalable platform.

**[Back to Overview]**

These are just the first iterations of what's possible. The designs will evolve, the content will expand, and the platform will adapt based on what we learn — feeding back into the AI Pulse.
